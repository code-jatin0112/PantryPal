# PantryPal

PantryPal is an AI-powered kitchen assistant designed to help users manage their pantry, discover meals based on the ingredients they already have, plan food according to their budget and serving requirements, and reduce avoidable food waste.

## Core Idea

> Use what you have before buying more.

## Planned Features

- Pantry and ingredient management
- Expiry tracking
- Pantry-based meal recommendations
- Serving-size adjustment
- Budget-aware meal planning
- Smart grocery lists
- AI kitchen assistant
- Step-by-step cooking mode
- Pantry usage tracking
